# R-42 decisions: Home, browse, buyer pages, notifications

Written 2026-11-08 by the discover workstream (branch `feat/r42-uid`). A separate file to avoid a merge conflict; fold it into `docs/R42_DECISIONS.md` when the branches merge. Rules that are not mine to set are under "Needs owner" and nothing was built for them.

## Taken

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| D-1 | Home is read from the person's own rows, not from a role. `ViewerActivity` answers "what is this person doing": bids, sales owed, reserve decisions, own lots, watches. One account can be buyer, seller and operator at once. | A seller who also bids must not see half a page. |
| D-2 | Home opens with "Needs you": one plain sentence per thing and a direct link. Order: reserve decision, overdue payment, payment due, outbid, leads closing within 24 h, a buyer late paying (seller), payments needing an operator. When nothing needs them it says so. A brand-new account (no bid, watch, lot or sale) gets "Two ways to start" and no zeros. Sections exist only with data. | Brief item 1. |
| D-3 | Browse states (`?state=`): live, ending (closing soon), scheduled, pending (awaiting decision), sold, unsold, cancelled. `live` includes the closing-soon lots, so the tabs overlap on purpose (a lot can be in Live and in Closing soon). The old `?status=active` still lands on Live. Counts per tab come from one grouped query for the current search and category. | The old filter put scheduled lots under "active" and could not say sold or awaiting decision. |
| D-4 | A lot whose seller rejected the top bid is **Not sold**, not Sold, in browse, filters, My bids and Won. `Display::lotState()` (shell) calls any ended lot with a winning bid "sold", which is wrong after a rejection (the winning bid row stays marked). It now reads a `reserve_rejected` column when a list loads it (LotBrowser does); where it is not loaded the old rule holds, so other pages (auction detail, owned by ui-auction) should load it too or call `LotBrowser::withViewerBid()`. | Found by checking the demo world's rejected lot; no rule invented, the data already says it. |
| D-5 | Sorting: ending soonest (default; open lots first, soonest close first, then scheduled, awaiting decision, closed by most recent close, cancelled last), newest, price low or high, most bids. Everything lives in the query string; unknown values fall back to the default. | Brief item 2. |
| D-6 | Lot cards show what the lot is to the viewer: You lead, Outbid, You won, Your lot (`ViewerCue`), computed from columns loaded in the list query. The Watch control is a new Livewire component, `Buyer\WatchToggle`, not the existing `WatchlistButton`: it takes its state from the list (no query per card), has a visible word ("Watch", "Stop watching") plus the lot title in its accessible name, and is not offered on your own lot, nor on closed lots you do not already watch. `WatchlistButton` is untouched (ui-auction owns the detail page). | Twelve cards must not cost twelve queries; the old button was a per-instance query. |
| D-7 | `lot-card` gained two optional things: `viewer` (the cue) and an `actions` slot. `empty-state` gained `level` so it can be an h2 under the h1. Both additive, defaults unchanged. | Needed by the pages; no other behaviour changed. |
| D-8 | Notification bell: opening a notification marks it read and follows its stored address; the list is all unread (up to 20) plus the 5 most recent read; "See all notifications" opens `/notifications` (paged, with Mark all as read). The stored address is only ever followed as a path on our own host (an absolute address on the app's host is reduced to its path; any other host, `//host`, control characters and backslashes are refused and the notification is only marked read). | A redirect from stored data must not be an open redirect, and a demo or `www` host must work. |
| D-9 | The bell asks for Echo only when `auction.realtime.enabled` is on, the default broadcaster is `reverb` and a Reverb key is configured (`NotificationBell::usesEcho()`), so Livewire never looks for a missing Echo (no console warning). Otherwise it polls every 30 s while the tab is visible (`wire:poll.30s.visible`; Livewire pauses hidden tabs). The `userId` it builds the channel name from is now locked against tampering. | Production runs without Reverb. |
| D-10 | Notification copy: no em dashes, no relative times that go stale. Outbid now says the price and the next valid bid; ending soon gives the closing time in South African time. The bell and inbox show the creation time through `Display::time()`. Already stored notifications keep their old text. | Voice rules in DESIGN.md. |
| D-11 | The dashboard is a controller (`DashboardController`), not a closure in `routes/web.php`, so it survives `route:cache`. Routes added in a `// --- discover ---` block: `dashboard`, `buyer.bids` (`/bids`), `buyer.watchlist` (`/watchlist`, plus `DELETE /watchlist/{auction}` for the no-JavaScript "Stop watching" form), `buyer.won` (`/won`), `notifications.index`, `notifications.open` (POST), `notifications.read-all` (POST). | Brief items 3 and 4. |
| D-12 | The AgriTech Intelligence Suite, the five metric tiles, and the unlinked category chips are deleted. Seven rules of `legacy.css` they used went with them. The reserve-decision panel (ui-auction's) is included on Home under its own heading and reachable from the Needs you line. | Brief items 1, 5, 6. |

## Needs owner (nothing built)

* **Won lots without a sale record.** A lot won through the settler normally has its sale within minutes (the hourly sweep repairs gaps). Until then Won says "The sale is being recorded." and offers the lot, not a pay button. If the owner wants a pay path that does not depend on the sale record, that is a payments rule.
* **A rejected reserve lot and its top bidder.** The bidder is told nothing when the seller rejects (backend decision F-09). My bids shows it as "Not sold", which is true but not an explanation; a notice to the bidder needs the owner's rule.
* **Watching a lot that has ended.** The card offers Stop watching but not Watch on closed lots. Whether a person may keep a watch on a closed lot (and be told its outcome) is a product choice.
* **Closing soon.** Browse uses the configured final-hour window (60 minutes), Home uses the next 24 hours. If the owner wants one window everywhere, say which.
